PHIRST PLAYER

Accessibility

Ruoy Young Corporation, operating the Phirst Player brand, is committed to making its website accessible and easier to use for as many people as possible.

Our Approach

We aim to use readable text, clear navigation, descriptive image text, and layouts that adapt to different screen sizes and devices. We continue to review the website and consider improvements to its accessibility.

Third-Party Content

Some features, including payment services and the embedded online game, may be provided by third parties. We cannot control every aspect of the accessibility of those external services, but we welcome feedback about barriers encountered while using them.

Need Assistance?

If you experience difficulty accessing any part of the website, need help completing a purchase, or would like to report an accessibility issue, contact us:

support@phirstplayer.com

Please describe the page or feature you were trying to use and the issue you encountered. We will make reasonable efforts to assist.
