Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ruoy Young Corporation, operating the Phirst Player brand, handles information when you visit our website, make a purchase, or subscribe to the Players Circle.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the website, information may include:

  • Email address submitted to the Players Circle.
  • Information you provide when contacting customer support.
  • Order and transaction information made available to us through the payment process.
  • Basic technical information that may be processed by website hosting or third-party services.

Players Circle Email Subscriptions

When you subscribe to the Players Circle, your email address is recorded in our subscription spreadsheet. The system may also record the subscription date and subscription status. We use this information to manage subscriptions and send the Players Circle welcome email and other requested brand updates.

You may request to stop receiving marketing emails by contacting support@phirstplayer.com. Marketing consent is not required to purchase a product.

Payments

Purchases on the website may be processed through PayPal. Payment information is submitted to PayPal and handled under PayPal's own privacy policy and security practices. We do not intend to collect or store your complete payment card number through the Phirst Player website.

How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Process and support customer orders.
  • Send subscription emails you have requested.
  • Respond to customer service inquiries.
  • Maintain website operations and security.
  • Meet applicable legal and business recordkeeping obligations.

Service Providers

Our website and business processes may rely on third-party services, including Google services for subscription administration and email delivery, PayPal for payment processing, website hosting providers, and the provider hosting our online game. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices.

Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information for money. We may share information with service providers when necessary to operate the website, process transactions, deliver requested communications, protect our business, or comply with law.

Data Retention and Security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including customer support, business records, and legal obligations. We use reasonable measures intended to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices and Requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we maintain, subject to applicable legal requirements and legitimate recordkeeping needs. You may also request to unsubscribe from marketing emails.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy as our website and services change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email support@phirstplayer.com.
